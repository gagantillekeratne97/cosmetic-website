Why "Serendib"

The old name for this island gave the world a word for happy accident.

"Serendib" is the old Persian and Arabic name for Sri Lanka — the root of the word serendipity, coined to describe travellers finding good things here they weren't looking for.

That's still roughly how the business runs. It started as a single spice stall supplying the hotel trade; the confectionery side began almost by accident, when the family started making holiday sweets from the same clove, cardamom and cinnamon in the storeroom. Customers started asking for the sweets more than the spice.

We kept both. Every jar and every box still comes out of the same building, weighed on the same scales, by the same three families who've run it for two generations.
